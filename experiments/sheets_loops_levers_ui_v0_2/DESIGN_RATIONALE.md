# Design rationale

## One mechanism, three mathematical roles

The iteration avoids treating depth, time, and action as interchangeable display dimensions.

| Chapter | Base | Fiber/object | Registered claim |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sheets | depth interval `[0,27]` | discrete target sheets with continuous section coordinates | local equivalence under global normalization and declared tolerance |
| Loops | closed phase `S¹` | transported association frame | return rotation under a registered connection |
| Levers | mechanism state | Gramian ellipsoid in the tangent/control fiber | finite-horizon reachable dimension and action efficiency |

The selected motif is the coordination key. Changing it in any chapter updates all chapters without pretending that the three bases are a single Euclidean space.

## Honest visual encodings

- The depth chart displays one section coordinate while local gluing is computed from full globally normalized `(x,y,z)` distances.
- The time animation rotates the transported frame; it does not rotate the camera and call that holonomy.
- The time base is explicitly closed. If a future dataset cannot certify phase closure, the loop chapter must report unavailable.
- The lever view shows exact synthetic rank but carries the experimental warning that measured Jacobians require singular-value uncertainty and abstention.
- Holonomy–control coupling is `||HWHᵀ-W||F / ||W||F`; it reports reassociation of reachable geometry, not additional reachability rank.

## Essay escalation

The chapter sequence deliberately raises the evidence bar:

1. local descriptive organization;
2. path-dependent transported association;
3. registered causal action geometry;
4. synthesis without claim collapse.

The synthesis view is a routing surface. It does not average the three certificates into a single score.

## Ablation as the connecting event

The worked example uses one operator rather than inventing a fourth geometric base:

`A(α) = I - αuuᵀ`.

It transforms every registered representation coherently: `hα=Aαh`, `aα=normalize(Aαa)`, and `Wα=AαWAαᵀ`. The behavior readout is applied to `hα`; it is not hand-switched by the slider. At full ablation the selected direction coefficient and its reachable energy collapse to zero while an orthogonal activation residual remains. That distinction keeps “targeted feature removal” separate from erasing the whole state.
